## Pairing basis

Each comparison pairs the two configurations run with the same seed in the same arm and capacity. The pairs share the seed, the shuffle order of the training instances, and part of the initial weights: the LSTM recurrent kernel, the Dense(8) kernel, the output kernel and all biases. **The LSTM input kernel and the first Dense kernel differ**, because their shapes depend on the number of input features, so the two members of a pair do not start from an identical model.

The shared part was checked from the stored hashes: in all 13 comparisons below, all 30 seeds give bit-identical initial values for every weight tensor whose shape is the same in both configurations.

## Method

- SD is the spread across the run-level MAEs (one MAE per initialization).
- Difference = PURE_ML - configuration; percent difference = (PURE_ML - configuration) / configuration x 100. **Positive means the configuration beats PURE_ML.** For FULL_HYBRID this has the same sign as the Pure-ML degradation in `capacity_summary.md`.
- Wilcoxon: two-sided signed-rank test on the 30 within-seed MAE differences.
- Holm: the FULL_HYBRID versus PURE_ML comparisons are corrected across the capacities within each arm (four in Arm A, four in Arm B, one in Arm B clean, where the corrected value equals the raw one). The four Arm B clean 4-unit configurations compared with PURE_ML are a separate family of four.
- Bootstrap intervals: percentile 95%, 10000 resamples of the 30 seeds (pairs kept together). They reflect variation across initializations on a fixed train/test split, not variation across patients.
- dz is the mean within-seed difference divided by the SD of the within-seed differences.
